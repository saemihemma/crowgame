Streak — the owl ring under load
Consecutive correct answers inside one level. It only ever adds: a wrong answer dims the flame to 40% and pauses it, it never resets. Reset happens on leaving the level, nothing else. Shown at 3× so the 6px stroke is legible.

Why it can only add. A streak that resets on a wrong answer turns the most confidence-sensitive moment in the game into a punishment. Dimming instead of resetting keeps the chase without the sting — and a child replaying a level to protect a streak is a child doing more maths.
The chime is the tell. Correct-answer pitch rises one semitone per step, capped at +5. One parameter, and it is the thing children actually chase. Never let it descend.
Budget. Streak 3 and 5 are T2 events (≤900ms, shake ≤0.004, ≤32 particles). Never fire two T2+ inside 600ms — queue the second. BRAND_SYSTEM §10.1.
